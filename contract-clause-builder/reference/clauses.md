# Clause set — Limitation of liability (Russian law, English wording)

The governing law is Russian. The wording below is English, for use in
English-language or bilingual drafts.

**Status of the English text.** Each clause is an English rendering of the
firm's Russian wording. The Russian original governs. The English has been
prepared for this project and is pending firm approval; the provenance labels
below describe the underlying Russian position, not the English rendering.

- **FROM AGREEMENT** — the position is taken from the firm's executed text
- **FROM AGREEMENT, ADAPTED** — the firm's position, restructured as noted
- **DRAFT** — a counterpart position, not yet approved

**Terms that must not be varied.** These carry the distinction between article
431.2 and article 406.1 of the Civil Code. Substituting English-law terminology
would change the legal meaning.

| Use | Never |
|---|---|
| Representations | warranties |
| compensation of losses | indemnity |
| property losses | — |
| Claim | — |

Square brackets mark values inserted from the rule table.

## LIM-SCOPE — Scope

**FROM AGREEMENT** (cl. 10.1). All positions except 4.
Requires: DEF-REPS, DEF-SCHEDULE-LOSSES, DEF-CLAIM

> The provisions of this clause [N] (Limitation of Liability), together with the
> other provisions of this Agreement limiting liability, limit the Seller's
> liability under or in connection with this Agreement in respect of any claim by
> the Buyer against the Seller arising from the inaccuracy of the Seller's
> Representations and/or the occurrence of any of the circumstances giving rise
> to compensation of losses listed in Schedule [N] (Circumstances Giving Rise to
> Compensation of Property Losses and the Method of Their Calculation) (a
> "Claim"), and establish the procedure for bringing and satisfying Claims.

## LIM-COND — Conditional claims

**FROM AGREEMENT** (cl. 10.5). All positions except 1.
Requires: one selected claim-period clause, LIM-TPC-NOTICE, DEF-APPLICABLE-LAW

> If a Claim arises from a Third Party Claim, or from the facts, events or
> circumstances set out in a Third Party Claim (a "Conditional Claim"), and:
>
> (i) the relevant Third Party Claim Notice was delivered to the Buyer within the
> periods specified in clauses [LIM-PERIODS] and [LIM-TPC-NOTICE], or the Third
> Party Claim was delivered to the Seller within those same periods; and
>
> (ii) as at the expiry of the periods specified in clause [LIM-PERIODS], the
> relevant Third Party Claim had not yet been confirmed by:
>
> (a) a judgment of a Russian court that has entered into legal force, following
> consideration of the case by a court of appeal;
>
> (b) a final award on the merits, having entered into legal force, of an
> arbitral tribunal or international commercial arbitration seated in the Russian
> Federation;
>
> (c) a final judgment, having entered into legal force, of a foreign court or
> foreign arbitral tribunal rendered in the territory of a foreign state and
> recognised and enforced in the Russian Federation (where such recognition and
> enforcement is required under Applicable Law);
>
> (d) a settlement agreement that has entered into legal force (where such
> settlement agreement is not subject to court approval under Applicable Law) or
> a settlement agreement approved by a court (where such approval is required
> under Applicable Law),
>
> then the relevant Conditional Claim may be delivered to the Seller within
> [CONDITIONAL PERIOD] from the date on which the relevant Third Party Claim was
> confirmed in accordance with this clause, and in such case satisfaction of that
> Conditional Claim may not be refused on the ground that it was brought after
> the periods specified in clause [LIM-PERIODS].

## LIM-CAP-PRICE — Aggregate cap at the purchase price

**FROM AGREEMENT** (cl. 10.6). Position 2 only.
Requires: DEF-PRICE, DEF-CLAIM

A single aggregate cap covering both breach of representations and compensation
of losses. The parenthesis is load-bearing — see consistency rule 2.

> The Seller's aggregate liability to the Buyer under this Agreement in respect
> of all Claims in aggregate (including, for the avoidance of doubt, property
> losses compensated by the Seller to the Buyer) shall not in any event exceed
> the amount of the Purchase Price for the Sale Shares actually received by the
> Seller.

## LIM-NODOUBLE — No double recovery

**FROM AGREEMENT** (cl. 10.8). All positions except 1.
Requires: DEF-SCHEDULE-LOSSES

> the extent that the damages and/or property losses listed in Schedule [N]
> (Circumstances Giving Rise to Compensation of Property Losses and the Method of
> Their Calculation) which are the subject of the relevant claim have already
> been paid to the Buyer under another equivalent claim.

## Note on the Russian law structure

Russian SPAs run two parallel tracks.

**Representations** (заверения об обстоятельствах, art. 431.2 Civil Code). A
breach gives rise to liability for damages.

**Compensation of losses** (возмещение потерь, art. 406.1) arises on the
occurrence of specified circumstances and is *not* liability for breach. It
arises whether or not any party has acted wrongfully.

The consequence for drafting: a clause capping "liability" does not
automatically reach compensation under art. 406.1. Every cap variant above
therefore says so expressly, through the parenthesis *including, for the
avoidance of doubt, property losses compensated by the Seller to the Buyer*.
Consistency rule 2 checks that it survives.
