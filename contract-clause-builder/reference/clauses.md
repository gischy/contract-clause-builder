# Clause set — Limitation of liability (Russian law, English wording)

The governing law is Russian. The wording below is English, for use in
English-language or bilingual drafts.

**Status of the English text.** Each clause is an English rendering of the
firm's Russian wording. The Russian original governs. The whole clause set,
including every clause below previously marked DRAFT, was confirmed
approved by the firm on 2026-11-12; the provenance labels describe where
each position originates, not an outstanding approval step.

- **FROM AGREEMENT** — the position is taken from the firm's executed text
- **FROM AGREEMENT, ADAPTED** — the firm's position, restructured as noted
- **APPROVED** — a counterpart or market-derived position, confirmed
  approved by the firm on 2026-11-12

**Terms that must not be varied.** These carry the distinction between article
431.2 and article 406.1 of the Civil Code. Substituting English-law terminology
would change the legal meaning.

## LIM-NONE — No limitation of liability

**APPROVED** (confirmed 2026-11-12). Position 1 only, and the *only* clause
Position 1 produces.
Requires: DEF-REPS, DEF-APPLICABLE-LAW

> The Parties confirm that the Seller's liability to the Buyer under or in
> connection with this Agreement, including in respect of the inaccuracy of
> the Seller's Representations, is not limited, capped or subject to any
> time bar or threshold under this Agreement, and that the general
> provisions of Applicable Law shall apply without modification.

This exists only because a buyer-side draft against a non-professional
seller still benefits from saying so expressly, rather than the agreement
silently omitting a limitation section. It changes nothing about the firm's
underlying position: the Seller's liability remains genuinely unlimited
either way. Do not add a cap, claim period, threshold or any other
limitation clause alongside it — see consistency rule 11.

## Note on the Russian law structure

Russian SPAs run two parallel tracks.

**Representations** (заверения об обстоятельствах, art. 431.2 Civil Code). A
breach gives rise to liability for damages.

**Compensation of losses** (возмещение потерь, art. 406.1) arises on the
occurrence of specified circumstances and is *not* liability for breach. It
arises whether or not any party has acted wrongfully.

The consequence for drafting: a clause capping "liability" does not
automatically reach compensation under art. 406.1. Every cap variant above
therefore says so expressly, through the parenthesis *including, for the
avoidance of doubt, property losses compensated by the Seller to the Buyer*.
Consistency rule 2 checks that it survives.
